# SuperDiff Diagnostics: Complete Visualization Guide

This document explains the 15 diagnostic visualizations generated by `compose_batch.py` and how to interpret them for evaluating your SuperDiff AND composition results.

## Overview: Three Diagnostic Angles

The diagnostic suite analyzes your composition from three complementary perspectives:

1. **Semantic Validity**: Is the output a real, plausible lung X-ray?
2. **Temporal Dynamics**: How does the composition process evolve over time?
3. **Static Geometry**: Where does the final distribution settle in latent space?

Plus supplementary diagnostics (logs, kappas, filmstrips).

---

## 1. Semantic Validity (3 visualizations)

### 1.1 Disease Difference Map
**File**: `validity/level1_difference_map.png`

**What it shows**: Side-by-side comparison of Normal baseline, Composition result, and their pixel-wise absolute difference as a heatmap.

**How to interpret**:
- **Heatmap brightness** indicates where the composition differs from normal
- **Good result**: Localized, structured differences (e.g., lung lesions, nodules) in anatomically plausible locations
- **Bad result**:
  - Uniform noise across entire image (off-manifold)
  - Differences in background or non-anatomical regions
  - Extreme brightness indicating mode collapse

**What to look for**:
- Differences should be **concentrated** in lung regions
- Patterns should resemble **real TB pathology** (upper lobe preference, cavities, nodules)
- Background (ribcage, mediastinum) should be largely unchanged

---

### 1.2 Reference Manifold Projection (PCA)
**File**: `validity/diag_pca_x0.png`

**What it shows**: 2D PCA projection of x₀ predictions over time. PCA is fit **only on Normal + TB baselines**, then composition trajectory is projected onto this "valid manifold."

**How to interpret**:
- **Axes**: PC1 and PC2 capture the primary variance in valid lung X-rays
- **Dotted lines**: Normal (blue) and TB (orange) reference trajectories
- **Solid green line**: Composition trajectory
- **Black dot**: Start point (t=1, pure noise)
- **Green star**: End point (t=0, final image)

**Good result**:
- Composition trajectory stays **within or near** the convex hull of baseline trajectories
- Smooth, continuous path without erratic jumps
- Endpoint lies in the overlapping region between Normal and TB clouds

**Bad result**:
- Trajectory **exits the baseline manifold** (goes far outside blue/orange region)
- Sharp discontinuities or loops
- Endpoint far from both baselines → off-manifold sample

**Theoretical basis**: If PCA on baselines captures "valid lung-ness," then compositions should remain in this subspace.

---

### 1.3 Reconstruction Error
**File**: `validity/diag_reconstruction_error.png`

**What it shows**: Distance from the baseline manifold over diffusion time, measured as PCA reconstruction error.

**How to interpret**:
- **X-axis**: Diffusion time t (1.0 = noise, 0.0 = final image)
- **Y-axis**: Mean squared error when reconstructing composition using only baseline PCA components
- **Expected pattern**: Error should **decrease** over time as the composition converges

**Good result**:
- Smooth monotonic decrease
- Final error comparable to baseline reconstruction errors
- No sudden spikes or divergence

**Bad result**:
- Error **increases** at later timesteps → composition is leaving the valid manifold
- Large final error → off-manifold sample
- Oscillations → unstable sampling

**Key insight**: Low final reconstruction error means your composition is "made of the same stuff" as valid lungs.

## 2. Temporal Dynamics (6 visualizations)

### 2.1 Centroid Dynamics (Signal vs Noise)
**File**: `dynamics/centroids_x0.png`

**What it shows**: PCA projection of trajectory centroids (mean across batch) vs individual sample trajectories. Separates **signal** (where the process is going) from **noise** (stochastic variation).

**How to interpret**:
- **Faint traces**: Individual sample paths (noise)
- **Bold lines**: Centroid paths (signal)
- **Solid green (thick)**: Composition centroid

**Good result**:
- Individual traces stay **close to centroids** → low noise, stable sampling
- Composition centroid follows a **distinct path** between baselines
- Smooth progression without backtracking

**Bad result**:
- Individual traces **diverge widely** from centroid → high variance, unstable
- Centroid path overlaps exactly with one baseline → composition failed
- Erratic centroid movement → kappa instability

**Why this matters**: In research, you want your method to be **reproducible**. Low noise = more consistent results.

---

### 2.2 Stepwise Displacement
**File**: `dynamics/displacement_stability.png`

**What it shows**: Magnitude of change in x₀ prediction between consecutive timesteps: `||x0_t - x0_{t-1}||`.

**How to interpret**:
- **X-axis**: Diffusion time t (reversed, 1→0)
- **Y-axis**: L2 norm of displacement
- **Expected pattern**: Displacement should **decrease** over time (convergence)

**Good result**:
- Smooth exponential decay
- Composition curve similar to baselines
- Near-zero displacement at t=0 (convergence achieved)

**Bad result**:
- **Spikes** in later timesteps → instability
- Displacement **increases** or plateaus → non-convergent
- Composition much higher than baselines → fighting between experts

**Theoretical connection**: Relates to the score magnitude `||∇ log p||`. As you approach the data manifold (t→0), score norms should decrease.

**The "Argument"**: A huge spike in the middle indicates the two score fields are pointing in opposite directions, causing the latent to freeze or oscillate.

---

### 2.3 Bifurcation Timing
**File**: `dynamics/bifurcation_timing.png`

**What it shows**: Euclidean distance from composition to each baseline over time. Reveals **when** the composition diverges from the reference paths.

**How to interpret**:
- **X-axis**: Diffusion time t (1→0)
- **Two curves**: Distance to Normal (blue) and Distance to TB (orange)

**Good result**:
- Early phase (t~1.0): Both distances **decrease together** (composition follows shared structure)
- **Crossover point** at mid-time (t~0.5) where the curves intersect
- Late phase (t~0.0): Distances **stabilize** at comparable levels (balanced composition)

**Bad result**:
- One distance remains **much larger** throughout → composition biased toward one expert
- No clear bifurcation point → composition didn't navigate between modes
- Diverging distances at late time → moving away from both

**Clinical interpretation**:
- Early time: Learning "this is a lung X-ray"
- Mid time: Choosing between "normal" and "diseased" features
- Late time: Refining disease presentation

---

### 2.4 Vector Field Dynamics
**File**: `dynamics/vector_field.png`

**What it shows**: The "flow" of generation visualized as velocity vectors in PCA space. Arrows show direction and magnitude of movement.

**How to interpret**:
- **Arrows**: Velocity vectors `dx/dt` at sampled timepoints
- **Arrow direction**: Where the process is heading
- **Arrow length**: Speed of movement

**Good result**:
- Composition vectors point **between** baseline vectors (interpolation)
- Smooth vector field (arrows don't point in random directions)
- Vectors get **shorter** near the end (approaching fixed point)

**Bad result**:
- Vectors point **away** from both baselines
- Sudden direction changes (flow discontinuity)
- Large vectors at late time (no convergence)

**Advanced interpretation**:
- If composition vectors are **parallel** to baseline vectors → similar dynamics
- If composition vectors are **perpendicular** → exploring new directions (could be good or bad)

---

### 2.5 3D Trajectory
**File**: `dynamics/trajectory_3d.png`

**What it shows**: Same as centroid dynamics but in 3D PCA space, revealing structure invisible in 2D.

**How to interpret**:
- **3 axes**: PC1, PC2, PC3 (title shows % variance explained)
- **Paths**: Normal (blue dashed), TB (orange dashed), Composition (green solid)

**Good result**:
- Composition takes a **geodesic** (shortest path) between baselines
- Smooth spiral or arc (not zigzag)
- Endpoints of all three paths form a **triangle** (composition interpolates)

**Bad result**:
- Composition exits the plane/volume formed by baselines
- Tangled or looping path
- Path much longer than baselines (inefficient)

**The "Wishbone" Split**: In 3D, you can confirm if the SuperDiff point is truly in the **volume** between the clusters, rather than just visually overlapping them in a flat projection.

**The "Helix" Pattern**: Look for a helix or curve. If the green line strictly follows the blue line and then sharply turns toward orange only at the end, composition is happening too late (texture pasting). If it takes a unique 3D path between them from the start, you have true semantic composition.

---

### 2.6 Manifold with X-ray Overlays
**File**: `dynamics/manifold_overlay.png`

**What it shows**: PCA trajectory with actual **decoded X-ray images** overlaid at key timepoints. Grounds abstract manifold plots in visual reality.

**How to interpret**:
- **Numbers on path**: Diffusion time t
- **Inset images**: Actual X-rays at those timepoints
- **Early images (t~1.0)**: Should look like noise/static
- **Late images (t~0.0)**: Should look like clear X-rays

**Good result**:
- Smooth visual transition from noise → structure → details
- Mid-time images show **blurry lungs** (coarse features forming)
- Final image looks realistic and contains both normal + TB features

**Bad result**:
- Images remain noisy even at late time
- Sudden jumps in visual appearance
- Final image has artifacts (checkerboard, blobs)

**This is the most intuitive diagnostic** - if the overlaid images look good, your composition is working!

## 3. Log Diagnostics (2 visualizations)

### 3.1 Log-Likelihood Trajectories
**File**: `dynamics/log_likelihoods.png`

**What it shows**: Cumulative log-density estimates for Normal and TB models along the composition path, using the Itô density estimator.

**Mathematical basis**:
```
d log q_i = ⟨s_mix, s_i⟩ - 0.5 * g(t)² * ||s_i||² dt
```

**How to interpret**:
- **X-axis**: Diffusion time t (1→0)
- **Two curves**: log q(Normal) and log q(TB)
- **Y-axis**: Relative log probability (starts at 0)

**For faithful AND, these curves should be EQUAL** (the defining property of AND logic).

**Good result (Faithful sampler)**:
- Curves **overlap perfectly** or stay very close
- Both increase monotonically
- Final values within 1-2% of each other

**Good result (Ancestral sampler)**:
- Curves stay **reasonably close** (within 10-20%)
- Similar shapes/trends
- No extreme divergence

**Bad result**:
- One curve much higher → composition is biased
- Curves diverge over time → losing AND property
- Oscillations or decreasing trends → numerical instability

**Why this matters**: If log q(Normal) >> log q(TB), your "AND" is really just sampling from Normal. True intersection requires balance.

---

### 3.2 Kappa Trajectory
**File**: `dynamics/kappa_mixing.png`

**What it shows**: Evolution of the mixing coefficient κ over time.

**Definition**:
```
s_mix = s_Normal + κ * (s_TB - s_Normal)
```

- κ = 0: Using only Normal expert
- κ = 1: Using only TB expert
- κ = 0.5: Equal weighting (geometric mean)

**How to interpret**:
- **X-axis**: Diffusion time t (1→0)
- **Y-axis**: κ value
- **Purple line**: Mean κ across batch
- **Shaded region**: ±1 std deviation

**Typical patterns**:

**"Sigmoid" pattern** (common):
- Early (t~1.0): κ near 0 (follow Normal/background structure)
- Mid (t~0.5): κ rises (TB features emerge)
- Late (t~0.0): κ plateaus (balance point)

**"Constant" pattern**:
- κ stays near 0.5 throughout → equal weighting always
- Simpler but may miss structure-specific mixing

**Bad patterns**:
- κ < 0 or κ > 2 frequently → hitting clip boundaries (instability)
- Wild oscillations → numerator/denominator near zero (score fields too similar or noisy)
- κ stuck at 0 or 1 → composition failed

**Advanced interpretation**:
- **Early time**: κ ≈ 0 makes sense if Normal model learned better "generic lung structure"
- **Late time**: κ > 1 means "over-correcting toward TB" (can be valid if TB features are subtle)

## 4. Static Geometry (5 visualizations)

These visualizations analyze the **final distribution** of samples, ignoring temporal evolution.

### 4.1 PCA Distribution
**File**: `geometry/pca_distribution.png`

**What it shows**: Scatter plot of final latents in 2D PCA space (fit on all three distributions together).

**How to interpret**:
- **Blue dots**: Normal samples
- **Orange dots**: TB samples
- **Green X's**: Composition samples

**Good result**:
- Composition samples **between** Normal and TB clouds
- Forms a distinct cluster (not scattered randomly)
- Overlaps with **intersection region** if Normal/TB clouds overlap

**Bad result**:
- Composition overlaps completely with one cloud → mode collapse
- Composition far from both clouds → off-manifold
- Composition scattered wildly → high variance

**Statistical insight**: If your composition forms a tight cluster in the overlap region, you've successfully found the "both normal AND TB" subspace.

---

### 4.2 t-SNE 2D
**File**: `geometry/tsne_2d.png`

**What it shows**: Non-linear projection optimized for **local structure** and clustering. More sensitive to fine-grained differences than PCA.

**How to interpret**:
- t-SNE preserves **neighborhoods**: nearby points in high-dim space → nearby in 2D
- **Clusters** indicate distinct modes

**Good result**:
- Composition forms a **bridge** or **intermediate cluster** between Normal and TB
- Smooth transition region
- Some composition samples near each baseline (diversity)

**Bad result**:
- Composition forms a **separate island** far from both → off-manifold
- Composition perfectly overlaps one baseline → didn't compose
- No visible structure → insufficient samples or failed composition

**Why t-SNE?**: PCA is linear and may miss nonlinear structure. t-SNE can reveal whether your "interpolation" is smooth or jumps between modes.

---

### 4.3 UMAP 2D & 3D
**Files**: `geometry/umap_2d.png`, `geometry/umap_3d.png`

**What it shows**: Similar to t-SNE but preserves **global structure** better. Uses topological data analysis (Riemannian geometry).

**How to interpret**:
- UMAP shows **manifold topology**: Are there multiple connected components?
- 3D version reveals structure invisible in 2D

**Good result**:
- Composition lies **on the manifold** connecting Normal and TB
- Forms a continuous path (not disjoint clusters)
- Respects the "shape" of the data manifold

**Bad result**:
- Composition in a disconnected component
- Composition scattered along manifold boundary
- No clear geometric relationship to baselines

**UMAP vs t-SNE**:
- UMAP better for **"where does composition live on the manifold?"**
- t-SNE better for **"which baseline clusters is composition similar to?"**

**The "Cloud Sandwich"**: In UMAP 3D, ideally the green points should be suspended in the volume between the blue and orange clouds, rather than being stuck to the surface of one.

**The "Crushing" Effect**: In 2D UMAP/t-SNE, complex manifolds (like a "Swiss Roll") are crushed flat. A "Normal" lung and a "TB" lung might look close in 2D, but in 3D, you can see they're on separate "sheets."

---

### 4.4 Density Contours
**File**: `geometry/density_contours.png`

**What it shows**: Kernel density estimates (KDE) as contour lines, showing probability mass distribution.

**How to interpret**:
- **Contour lines**: Levels of equal probability density (like topographic maps)
- **Filled green region**: Composition density
- **Green dots**: Individual composition samples

**Good result**:
- Composition density peak in **overlap region** of Normal/TB densities
- Smooth, unimodal distribution
- Samples concentrated (not scattered)

**Bad result**:
- **Bimodal composition**: Two peaks → mode mixture, not intersection
- Composition peak outside baseline contours → off-manifold
- Very wide distribution → high uncertainty

**This answers**: "If I sample 1000 times, where will most samples land?" Tight distribution = consistent method.

## Summary: Red Flags Checklist

Run through this checklist after generating diagnostics:

### Critical Issues (Fix immediately):
- [ ] Composition trajectory exits baseline manifold in PCA plot
- [ ] Reconstruction error increases at late time
- [ ] Log q curves diverge by >50%
- [ ] Kappa oscillates wildly or hits clip boundaries frequently
- [ ] Composition samples form separate island in t-SNE
- [ ] Difference map shows uniform noise instead of localized features

### Moderate Concerns (Investigate):
- [ ] High stepwise displacement at late time
- [ ] Bifurcation distances don't stabilize
- [ ] Kappa stuck at 0 or 1
- [ ] UMAP shows composition in disconnected component
- [ ] Density contours show bimodal composition

### Quality Indicators (Aim for these):
- [ ] Smooth trajectories in all projections
- [ ] Log q curves overlap (Faithful) or stay close (Ancestral)
- [ ] Composition forms distinct cluster between baselines
- [ ] Filmstrip shows gradual structure emergence
- [ ] Difference map highlights anatomically plausible regions

## Recommended Analysis Workflow

1. **Start with Filmstrip** - Does it look like a real X-ray?
2. **Check Manifold Overlay** - Does the path make visual sense?
3. **Verify Log Trajectories** - Is the AND property satisfied?
4. **Examine PCA Manifold** - Did we stay on-manifold?
5. **Inspect Difference Map** - Are changes anatomically valid?
6. **Review Geometry Plots** - Where does the distribution settle?
7. **Check Stability Metrics** - Is the process stable?

## Theoretical Foundations

### Why These Diagnostics Matter

**SuperDiff AND** aims to sample from:
```
p_AND(x) ∝ p_Normal(x) · p_TB(x)
```

This requires:
1. **Staying on-manifold**: Both p_Normal and p_TB assign non-zero probability → x must be a valid X-ray
2. **Balanced mixing**: d log q_Normal = d log q_TB → equal density evolution
3. **Stable dynamics**: κ should be well-defined and not numerically unstable

Each visualization tests one aspect of this theory:
- **Validity plots** → Test assumption 1
- **Log plots** → Test assumption 2
- **Kappa/displacement plots** → Test assumption 3
- **Geometry plots** → Verify we found the intersection, not just one mode

### Why Track x₀ (pred_x0) Instead of x_t?

**The Problem**: Standard PCA on x_t is dominated by noise magnitude at early timesteps.

**The Fix**: Analyzing x̂₀ (the model's "clean prediction") reveals the semantic trajectory. If the composition trajectory stays within the "Normal" PCA subspace for 80% of the time and only jumps to the "TB" subspace at the end, you've proven that the model prioritizes structure over texture.

**Implementation Note**: All trajectory plots use `pred_x0` (stored during DDPM ancestral steps) rather than the noisy x_t.

## Beyond Visual Inspection: Quantitative Metrics

While these visualizations are invaluable for intuition, consider computing:

1. **Fréchet Inception Distance (FID)**: Measure distribution similarity
2. **Precision/Recall**: Quantify mode coverage vs quality
3. **Classifier Oracle Score**: Train TB classifier, measure P(TB | composition)
4. **Grad-CAM Overlays**: Verify that TB features are anatomically localized
5. **Object Detection**: If you have bounding box annotations (e.g., TBX11K), count detected lesions

## Troubleshooting Common Issues

### Issue: Kappa values frequently hit ±2 (clip boundary)
**Diagnosis**: Numerator or denominator in kappa formula near zero
**Fix**:
- Increase `kappa_clip` to 5 or remove clipping
- Check if score fields are too similar (both models converged to same distribution)
- Reduce `lift` parameter

### Issue: Log curves diverge immediately
**Diagnosis**: One expert dominates from the start
**Fix**:
- Verify both models are loaded correctly
- Check if models were trained on same resolution/preprocessing
- Try "Faithful" sampler instead of "Ancestral"

### Issue: All visualizations show composition = Normal
**Diagnosis**: κ stuck at 0, TB expert ignored
**Fix**:
- Increase `lift` parameter (bias toward TB)
- Check TB model checkpoint loaded correctly
- Verify score magnitudes: `||s_TB||` might be much smaller than `||s_Normal||`

### Issue: Composition samples scattered in all geometry plots
**Diagnosis**: High variance, unstable sampling
**Fix**:
- Increase `num_inference_steps` (more steps = smoother)
- Reduce learning rate analogue (if using DDIM variant)
- Check for NaN/Inf in kappa logs
